# {{name}}: architecture

A short frame: fill it in as work goes on and record decisions here. Do not invent anything in advance.

## Triggers
What starts the work: a schedule, a message, a webhook.

## Steps
The sequence of steps and what each one reads and writes.

## Secrets

## Failures and retries

What the agent may do, what needs a human's confirmation, spending limits.
